# Privacy Policy for VeroStat: Status Window
**Last Updated:** November 2026

VeroStat: Status Window ("the App") is a productivity and gamification platform. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data to comply with Google Play Console requirements.

### 1. Data We Collect
* **Account Data:** We collect your mail address and a hashed password strictly for secure account authentication via Supabase.
* **Location Data:** The App utilizes device GPS to verify "Geofence" daily tasks. Location data is processed locally on your device to calculate distance. We do not store your physical coordinates in our database or share them with third parties.
* **User-Generated Content:** Task names, financial goals (Logistics), and custom protocols are stored securely in our database to sync across your devices.

### 2. Third-Party AI Integration (Google Gemini)
To power the system AI, the App transmits the specific text of your daily goals and habits to Google Gemini via API. This data is strictly used to generate customized in-game attributes (XP, stats) and is governed by Google's API privacy standards.

### 3. Data Encryption
All data transmitted between the App and our servers (Supabase) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard cryptography (HTTPS/WSS).

### 4. Account and Data Deletion
You have the right to request the complete erasure of your account and all associated data. You can initiate this deletion by:
1. Deleting your account from within the App's "Settings" menu (System Factory Reset).
2. Emailing the developer directly at [Mrd36915@gmail.com] with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will manually purge your data within 7 days.
